The Real Problem
Your Front Desk Is Costing You Patients
These five problems drain revenue from chiropractic clinics every month. Most practice owners do not track them.
01

Missed Calls Go to Competitors

When a new patient calls and nobody picks up, they do not leave a voicemail. They call the next chiropractor on the list. One missed call during a busy afternoon is one new patient your competitor gets instead.

Avg. loss: $1,200-$3,600/month
02

No-Shows Destroy Your Schedule

Without automated reminders, your no-show rate climbs to 20-30%. That means empty adjustment slots, lost revenue, and a schedule full of gaps your front desk scrambles to fill.

Avg. loss: $2,000-$5,000/month
03

Weak Reviews Push Patients Away

Prospective patients check your Google reviews before they call. If you have fewer reviews or a lower rating than the clinic down the street, they choose the other clinic. Your team is too busy to ask every patient for a review.

Avg. loss: $2,000-$6,000/month
04

Inactive Patients Never Come Back

Patients who completed their treatment plan 90 days ago are not coming back on their own. Without a reactivation system, that revenue sits on the table. You already paid to acquire them.

Avg. loss: $3,000-$6,000/quarter
05

Your Front Desk Is Overwhelmed

Your staff is answering phones, checking patients in, handling insurance, scheduling follow-ups, and trying to collect reviews all at once. Calls go to voicemail. Follow-ups get delayed. Leads slip through.
